# Blur Basketball

## Elite Youth Basketball Development

Blur Basketball is a competitive AAU basketball program designed for athletes in grades 4-11 who are committed to excellence on and off the court. We combine elite-level training with a proven tournament schedule to accelerate player development through the Blur Development Model (BDM).

---

## The Blur Development Model (BDM)

The Blur Development Model is our proprietary framework for athlete progression. Rather than simply running drills, we build complete basketball players through:

- **Skill Mastery**: Position-specific fundamentals taught with precision
- **Game IQ Development**: Reading defenses, understanding spacing, making high-level decisions
- **Competitive Exposure**: Strategic tournament placement to showcase growth
- **Character Building**: Leadership, accountability, and team-first mentality

BDM isn't about shortcuts—it's about building athletes who are prepared for the next level, whether that's high school varsity, college recruitment, or beyond.
